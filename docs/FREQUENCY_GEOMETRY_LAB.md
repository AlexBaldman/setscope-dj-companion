# SetScope Frequency Geometry Lab

## The Category

SetScope should not become a shelf of audio-reactive screensavers. Its distinctive category is:

> **Reversible musical reasoning:** predict a consequence, make a reversible move, hear and see what changed, discover what stayed invariant, and transfer the insight to a real musical moment.

This is where the DJ companion, music school, science museum, puzzle game, and instrument finally become one product. Geometry is not decoration applied to sound. It is a manipulable representation of a relationship the player can test.

The creative north star is the feeling of a guided mathematical revelation: an ordinary gesture opens a door, a hidden relationship becomes audible, and the player can use it immediately. The work may draw inspiration from mathematical adventure films, mechanical puzzles, scientific instruments, records, samplers, oscilloscopes, harmonographs, and historical diagrams, but it must use original characters, worlds, art, rules, and language.

## The Truth Ledger

Every experiment, saved result, visualization, and share card carries one prominent truth label:

| Label | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `MEASURED` | Captured from a device sensor with method, calibration state, and uncertainty | A microphone recording's sampled spectrum |
| `INTERNAL DIGITAL` | Known signal generated and analyzed inside SetScope | Two oscillators producing a measured beat envelope |
| `MODEL` | A computed idealization with declared assumptions | An ideal string or vibrating plate eigenmode |
| `MAPPING` | A user-authored or designed translation between unlike domains | Timbre mapped to color or vibration |
| `STORY` | Symbolic, historical, contemplative, or poetic interpretation | A mandala used as a narrative frame for cyclic return |

These categories may converse, but they never silently collapse into one another. Sound and light both admit frequency descriptions, yet sound in air is a mechanical pressure wave and visible light is electromagnetic radiation. Mapping one to the other can be artistically and pedagogically rich; it is not a physical frequency identity.

“Sacred geometry” belongs in `STORY` unless a specific statement is independently mathematical or measured. SetScope can explore symmetry, proportion, tiling, recursion, and cultural symbolism without claiming that a shape heals, proves cosmic causation, or reveals a universal consciousness frequency.

## Research Footing

This direction has real scientific and mathematical material beneath it:

- Chladni figures are nodal patterns of vibrating plates governed by boundary conditions and plate eigenmodes, not evidence that sound reveals supernatural symbols. [Gander and Kwok, SIAM Review](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/10081931X)
- Chords can be represented as points in quotient/orbifold spaces and voice leadings as paths through those spaces. This is a powerful analytical lens, not the entirety of harmony. [Tymoczko, Science](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16825563/)
- The Euclidean algorithm can generate many maximally even onset patterns found in musical timelines. Evenness is a property, not a universal measure of groove or a claim of cultural invention. [Toussaint, Bridges 2005](https://archive.bridgesmathart.org/2005/bridges2005-47.html)
- Musical vibrotactile signals have been associated with changes in reported groove, engagement, and live-performance feeling. These ratings support experience claims, not learning claims. [Scientific Reports study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38565622/)
- Recent work on translating music to touch suggests the temporal envelope is an important carrier for tactile judgments of qualities such as roughness and pleasantness. It does not establish universal tactile meanings. [Translating Music to Touch](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41973576/)
- Instrument timbre can influence population-level color choices, but the relationships are complex and variable. SetScope should make individual authorship and calibration a feature. [Frontiers in Psychology](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1520131/full)
- Lissajous figures provide a direct visual demonstration of frequency ratio and relative phase. A closed figure demonstrates a rational ratio in an ideal system; it does not prove consonance or spiritual superiority. [Lissajous music pedagogy](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1191841)
- Recent generalized Tonnetz and tessellation research offers provocative compositional structures, but these are current mathematical explorations rather than settled maps of cognition. [Boland and Hughston, 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19960)

## The Preordained Synthesis: Route Resonator

The flagship experiment is **Route Resonator**.

A real Route junction opens into a compact seven-tile signal machine. Two rights-clean loops circulate through rotatable hexagonal junctions. The player first commits to a prediction such as:

> Will this move make the handoff feel earlier, heavier, or more suspended?

Each move reroutes conserved packets representing pulse, spectral weight, phrase position, or energy envelope. Nothing is created by a decorative flourish; the audible result follows from the route. Undo is exact. The completed move becomes an alternate transition, and transfer returns the player to a second junction where the same principle must be recognized or performed without copying the solution.

Why this is the center:

- **DJ relevance:** the source and destination are real transition decisions.
- **Puzzle integrity:** operations are reversible and quantities are conserved.
- **Musical learning:** prediction, consequence, explanation, and transfer are distinct events.
- **Visual identity:** Night Transit, Block Signal Broadcast, vinyl grooves, and illuminated signal hardware become functional rather than ornamental.
- **Architecture:** versioned challenges, deterministic replay, evidence, and Route context are exactly the primitives already prioritized by the Revolution Sprint.

First wedge: seven tiles, two generated loops, three packet types, rotate/undo/replay, one prediction prompt, and one transfer junction. Do not add procedural worlds or social sharing until this is delightful and causally legible.

## The First Constellation

### 1. Phase Loom

A toroidal field of oscillators can be shifted by row, column, or diagonal. The frequency inventory and total phase winding remain invariant while synchrony, cancellation, beating, and groove change.

The immediate lab form uses two oscillator arms and an XY trace with `1:1`, `2:1`, and `3:2` ratio locks. The game form expands to a `4 x 4` phase puzzle. Players predict the direction of change before hearing it and transfer at a new tempo.

**Why now:** astonishing output from tiny rules, a natural Audio Lab extension, and a strong demonstration that relationships matter more than isolated sounds.

### 2. Pulse Rosette

Place `k` attacks around an `n`-pulse ring, then rotate, reflect, split, merge, or slide them. Missions teach cyclic displacement, grouping, density, symmetry, clave direction, and maximally even spacing.

The rosette stamps its pattern into Rhythm Roulette's sequencer and performs it on the audio clock. Cultural provenance travels with any historical pattern; Euclidean construction is presented as an analytical generator, not an origin story or groove score.

**Why now:** the cheapest honest mathematical upgrade to Rhythm Roulette and a proving ground for reversible transformations.

### 3. Fourier Timbre Forge

An industrial partial press lets the player add, remove, and balance harmonic components, predict the resulting spectrum, and reconstruct a target by ear. Waveform, spectrum, and sound remain synchronized.

Every analysis card includes sample rate, FFT size, window type, frequency resolution, clipping state, and source label. Phase and transient behavior are introduced after amplitude spectra are understood.

**Why now:** musically useful on day one and supplies the spectral infrastructure needed by later labs.

### 4. Beat Loom

Tune two oscillators and predict the beat rate `f_b = |f_1 - f_2|`. Then match a rhythmic pulse to the amplitude envelope and transfer the concept to tuning, phasing, or a transition texture.

**Why now:** the smallest complete `Hypothesis -> Prediction -> Measurement -> Residual -> Transfer` experiment.

### 5. Harmonic Lock

Concentric rings redistribute small frequency ratios while preserving voice count and a declared product or geometric-mean invariant. Beating slows and interference rosettes sharpen as partials align. Alternate tonics and temperaments prevent one tuning system from being framed as morally or cosmically correct.

### 6. Orbifold Courier

Move individual chord voices through a folded transit city under a motion budget. Common tones save fuel; octave and voice-permutation equivalences fold boundaries. The chosen distance metric is always named as one analytical lens.

### 7. Temperament Vault

A tactile `3 x 4` pitch-class vault uses reversible row, column, and ring generators. The player solves target sonorities in few moves, discovers reachability invariants, and auditions every state. It is inspired by the elegance of mechanical permutation puzzles without copying a branded cube's appearance or move system.

### 8. Resonance Bridge

Strike a virtual resonator after moving excitation and coupling points. Players predict nodes, antinodes, damping, and ringing regions. The simulation is labeled `MODEL`; an optional later sensor activity can compare ideal and measured behavior.

### 9. Chladni Field Log

This is a protocol for a real, separate plate or membrane apparatus, not a fake cymatics generator. SetScope provides a conservative tone source, sweep log, photo annotation, apparatus notes, repeat runs, and error discussion. Rendered eigenmodes remain clearly marked `MODEL`; photographs are `MEASURED` only with instrumentation metadata.

### 10. Mapping Forge

Players author translations from onset, duration, density, boundary, and amplitude envelope into light, shape, motion, or haptic motifs. They then run identification, delayed recall, and transfer rounds against their own, randomized, and system-suggested mappings.

This is foundational accessibility infrastructure. It treats mappings as personal dialects instead of declaring that red means danger, upward means good, or one vibration universally means release.

### 11. Phrase Loom And Missing-Bar Switchboard

Players weave a multimodal envelope over a phrase, predict its next boundary, and progressively choose which information paths remain during a missing bar. Reveal is always available. Success is not defined as graduating toward “pure hearing”; visual, tactile, textual, spatial, and auditory paths remain first-class.

### 12. Spectra Are Not Colors

The player first learns additive display color, linear-light versus gamma-encoded mixing, and the physical difference between sound and light. Only then do they author an explicitly arbitrary spectrum-to-color instrument. The lesson turns a common category error into creative agency.

### 13. Comma Garden

Walk a 5-limit tuning lattice by `3/2` and `5/4`, attempt to close loops, hear the resulting commas, and compare temperament strategies. Just intonation is not called “pure music,” and equal temperament is not framed as defective.

### 14. Orbit Press

Two pendulum ratios create a live harmonograph trace that closes only when their periods reunite. The trace is evidence of the chosen ratio and phase, not ornamental wallpaper. Players predict reunion time, then carry the ratio into a polyrhythm.

### 15. Echo Fold

Fold a time strip across legal creases to create canon, retrograde, overlap, and call-and-response. Event inventory and unfolded duration remain invariant. Every fold is reversible and scheduled on exact audio-clock boundaries.

## A Guided Wonder Mode

The concepts should eventually be connected by an original short-form mathematical adventure. A young field engineer and a stubborn test signal repair a neighborhood broadcast rig by discovering partials, locking a Lissajous loop, spreading rhythm around a cycle, navigating chord space, and routing a transition home.

The narrative rhythm is:

1. Encounter an apparently magical behavior.
2. Commit a prediction.
3. Manipulate a tiny rule system.
4. Reveal the invariant.
5. Hear the result as music.
6. Use it in the Route.

Wonder comes from earned comprehension. The story never says that all domains are secretly one physical force; it shows that mathematical descriptions can reveal resonances between different systems without erasing their differences.

## Shared Engine, Not Universal Engine

Build a small reusable layer beneath Phase Loom and Pulse Rosette, then prove Route Resonator or Orbifold Courier can reuse it:

- `CyclicObject`: canonical pitch, rhythm, phase, or time coordinates with a modulus and declared equivalence policy.
- `Transformation`: pure reversible operation with stable id, parameters, inverse, and cost.
- `WorldGraph`: neighbors, reachability, shortest path, invariants, and target predicates.
- `DeterministicRun`: seed, initial state, timestamped actions, reducer, replay hash, and assistance metadata.
- `VoiceBank`: audio-clock scheduling for frequency, amplitude, phase, envelope, and conservative gain.
- `MathView`: circle, lattice, tiling, and trace projections with semantic DOM summaries.
- `Evidence`: locked prediction, action trace, mathematical result, musical result, confidence, residual, and transfer link.
- `MappingProfile`: author, provenance, device, modality parameters, calibration bounds, personal meaning, body location, assistance, and revocation.

Do not build a speculative universal math engine. The Revolution Sprint's contract spine and deterministic Pitch Gates remain prerequisites because the current render-timed, unseeded game logic cannot support scientific or learning claims.

## Virtual Lab Contract

Every experiment follows:

```text
Hypothesis
  -> locked numeric, categorical, or sketched prediction
  -> instrument and calibration card
  -> manipulation
  -> measurement or model result
  -> residual and error discussion
  -> musical transfer
```

Every result records, where applicable:

- Truth label and model assumptions.
- Source route and processing flags.
- Sample rate, FFT size, window, and frequency resolution.
- Device and actuator identity.
- Calibration state, latency estimate, and comfort bounds.
- Repeat count and uncertainty.
- Assistance and reveal history.
- Prediction timestamp before feedback.
- Transfer material and outcome.

## Learning Evidence Standard

An enjoyable or engaging experience is not automatically a learning intervention. Public “helps learning” claims require a preregistered primary outcome, counterbalanced controls, recorded calibration and latency, prediction before feedback, delayed retention, transfer to new material, effect sizes with uncertainty, participant-level heterogeneity, and confirmatory replication.

Deaf and hard-of-hearing musicians must be compensated co-authors of mapping tasks, success definitions, and interpretation, not late-stage accessibility testers. Early product language should remain bounded:

> SetScope helps players discover and test musical mappings that may work for them.

## Safety Baseline

- Generated sound starts muted, ramps over at least 20 ms, and uses a conservative digital ceiling. Digital level never masquerades as acoustic SPL.
- Sustained tones and sweeps are short, speaker-first, never ultrasonic or infrasonic, and stop immediately at discomfort or speaker rattle.
- A live microphone is never monitored into the same speakers by default.
- Chladni work uses a separate stable transducer and apparatus; the phone is a controller and camera, not part of the vibrating surface.
- Light fields are static or slowly changing by default. No strobe; motion and brightness have accessible limits.
- Vibration begins below the user's comfortable range and requires calibration, immediate stop, and short trials.
- Raw microphone and camera media remain local and ephemeral unless explicitly saved.

## Claims SetScope Must Never Make

- A frequency such as 432 Hz or 528 Hz heals, detoxifies, repairs DNA, treats illness, or reliably changes consciousness.
- Earth, planets, chakras, organs, emotions, or monuments possess one musically meaningful “true frequency.”
- Chladni patterns prove sacred geometry, intention, language, spirituality, or cosmic order.
- A rendered model is a measured physical pattern.
- Phone RMS is dB SPL or certifies hearing safety.
- One-decimal pitch display implies laboratory accuracy without calibration and uncertainty.
- Audio frequencies physically become visible colors through octave multiplication.
- Phase inversion eliminates sound throughout a room.
- Resonance creates unlimited energy.
- A detector objectively knows emotion, crowd response, culture, or musical intention.
